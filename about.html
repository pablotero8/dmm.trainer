The Philosophy ABOUT DMM TRAINER

What sets DMM Trainer apart from other online personal trainers, especially in the world of racquet sports?

I work in three-month cycles because progress requires structure, follow-up, and time. Each trimester is carefully planned to help you advance measurably and consistently. It's scientifically proven that 12 weeks of planned training is necessary for significant changes in our bodies.

Specialized in Racquet Sports

Being specialized in racquet sports means I understand not only the physical demands of these sports but also the technique, injury prevention, and specific preparation you need to perform at your best on the court. Whether you're an amateur or a competitive player, I'll adapt your training.
